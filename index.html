About JPI

Bridging Indonesian
Producers to APAC Buyers

Jakarta Portal International is a Jakarta-based general trading company specializing in the export and import of raw materials and commodities. Founded on deep knowledge of Indonesian regulatory frameworks and APAC trade routes, we serve as the operational bridge between Indonesia's world-class commodity producers and high-demand buyers across Asia.

Our team navigates Indonesia's complex export landscape — from Bea Cukai compliance and Ministry of Trade licensing to RCEP duty optimization — so your shipments move efficiently and fully compliant.

Reliability

Consistent on-time delivery and transparent communication at every stage.

Compliance

Full adherence to Indonesian export law and APAC import regulations.

Speed

Streamlined documentation and pre-cleared customs channels.

Partnership

Long-term relationships built on trust, transparency, and shared success.

What We Trade

Indonesia's Core Commodities

From agricultural staples to industrial minerals — we handle the full spectrum of Indonesia's most valuable export commodities with precision and compliance.

Palm Oil & Derivatives

Crude Palm Oil (CPO), Refined Bleached Deodorized (RBD) Palm Oil, Palm Kernel Oil, and oleochemical derivatives. Indonesia leads global supply — we connect you to the source.

HS 1511 · 1513

Coal & Minerals

Thermal and coking coal from Kalimantan, nickel ore from Sulawesi, tin concentrate from Bangka Belitung. Full documentation per Indonesian mining export regulations.

HS 2701 · 2604 · 2609

Natural Rubber

Technically Specified Rubber (TSR), Latex Concentrate, and Ribbed Smoked Sheets (RSS) from Sumatra and Kalimantan. Certified for automotive, medical, and industrial use.

HS 4001 · 4003

Cocoa & Coffee

Cocoa beans, cocoa butter, and cocoa powder from Sulawesi and Flores. Arabica and Robusta coffee. Single-origin specialty grades available with full traceability.

HS 1801 · 0901

Seafood & Marine Products

Fresh, frozen, and processed shrimp, tuna, skipjack, and crab. HACCP-certified processing facilities. Cold chain logistics coordinated port-to-port.

HS 0302 · 0306 · 1604

Industrial Raw Materials

Silica sand, limestone, wood pulp, bauxite, and steel scrap. We source from vetted Indonesian producers and handle all pre-shipment inspection requirements.

HS 2505 · 4703 · 2606

What We Do

End-to-End Trade Services

From first inquiry to final delivery — we manage every step of the trade process so you can focus on your core business.

01

Export Documentation

Complete preparation and verification of all export documents to ensure smooth customs clearance at origin and destination.

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (COO/SKA)
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) coordination
  • Phytosanitary & health certificates
02

Import Compliance & Customs

Destination-side compliance management across all eight APAC markets, with in-country agent networks for seamless customs clearance.

  • Customs tariff classification
  • Import license facilitation
  • Quarantine & inspection support
  • Duty drawback advisory
03

HS Code & Duty Optimization

Strategic HS code classification and FTA utilization to legally minimize duty exposure under RCEP, ASEAN FTAs, and bilateral agreements.

  • RCEP preference certificate
  • ASEAN FTA Form D & E
  • Binding ruling consultation
  • Duty rate comparison matrix
04

Trade Finance Support

Structuring and coordination of trade finance instruments to protect both buyers and sellers throughout the transaction lifecycle.

  • Letter of Credit (L/C) management
  • Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) coordination
  • Documents against Payment (D/P)
  • Bank guarantee facilitation
05

Supplier & Buyer Sourcing

Leveraging our vetted network of Indonesian commodity producers and APAC importers to match you with the right counterparty at the right terms.

  • Indonesian producer due diligence
  • APAC buyer qualification
  • Contract negotiation support
  • Long-term supply agreement structuring
06

APAC Market Entry Consulting

Strategic advisory for companies entering the Indonesian export market or sourcing commodities from Indonesia for the first time.

  • Market feasibility & pricing analysis
  • Regulatory roadmap per country
  • Logistics & port route optimization
  • Local partner identification

Compliance Framework

Bea Cukai — Indonesian Customs Authority compliance for all export declarations (PEB/PIB)

Ministry of Trade (Kemendag) — Export licensing (PE/ET) and commodity quota management

BPOM — Food safety certification for agricultural and marine exports

ESDM — Energy & mineral resources export compliance (nickel, coal, bauxite)

RCEP & ASEAN FTA — Form D/E/RCEP certificate of origin issuance

Pre-Shipment Inspection — LSI/PSI coordination per commodity type

ISPM 15 — Phytosanitary wood packaging compliance for agricultural commodities
