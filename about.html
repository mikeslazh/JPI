Company History

A Journey Built on Trust

Jakarta Portal International (JPI) was established in 2014 with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between Indonesia's vast natural resources and the markets that demand them. Founded by a team of seasoned trading professionals with deep roots in energy and commodity sectors, JPI quickly established itself as a reliable and compliant trading partner.

In our early years, we focused on coal and palm oil trading in Sumatra and Kalimantan. As trust grew, so did our portfolio. By 2017, we had expanded into natural gas distribution, rubber, and mineral trading — forging partnerships with producers, refiners, and logistics operators across 15 Indonesian provinces.

Today, JPI stands as a comprehensive general trading company, executing thousands of transactions annually across both the energy and commodity sectors. We remain privately held, independently operated, and deeply committed to the principles that built our reputation: integrity, transparency, and excellence in every deal.
