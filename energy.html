What We Trade

Energy Products

Comprehensive energy commodity trading across Indonesia's key resource sectors, with logistics and compliance support from source to delivery.

Coal

Indonesia is one of the world's largest coal exporters, and JPI is deeply embedded in this sector. We trade thermal and coking coal sourced from major mining regions, connecting producers to domestic power plants and international buyers.

  • Thermal coal (sub-bituminous & bituminous)
  • Coking coal for steel production
  • Caloric value: 4,000 – 7,000 kcal/kg range
  • Sources: Kalimantan, South Sumatra
  • Full documentation & ESDM compliance

Natural Gas

As Indonesia accelerates its transition to cleaner energy, natural gas plays a pivotal role. JPI facilitates gas trading, supply agreements, and LNG distribution, serving industrial clients, utilities, and downstream processors.

  • Pipeline gas supply agreements
  • Compressed Natural Gas (CNG)
  • Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG)
  • Sources: Natuna Sea, East Kalimantan
  • Industrial & commercial offtake contracts

Petroleum Products

JPI trades a range of downstream petroleum products including fuel oil, diesel, aviation fuel, and base oils, serving industrial operators, maritime fleets, and construction companies across the archipelago.

  • Marine fuel oil (MFO) & diesel (HSD)
  • Aviation turbine fuel (Avtur)
  • Industrial lubricant base oils
  • B30 biodiesel blended fuel
  • BPH Migas registered & compliant

Geothermal Energy

Indonesia sits atop the world's largest geothermal reserves. JPI actively participates in the geothermal sector through energy offtake advisory, investment facilitation, and trading services related to geothermal power projects.

  • Geothermal energy offtake advisory
  • Project development facilitation
  • Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) support
  • Regions: Java, Sumatra, Sulawesi
  • Collaboration with PLN & IPPs

Energy Coverage

Nationwide Energy Distribution

JPI's energy operations span Indonesia's most resource-rich provinces. Our coal trading network connects mining sites in Kalimantan and South Sumatra to power generators across Java and export terminals in Balikpapan, Samarinda, and Tarahan.

Our petroleum and gas trading activities are supported by partnerships with certified logistics operators, ensuring last-mile delivery to remote industrial sites, maritime operators, and island territories.

In the geothermal sector, JPI is active in project facilitation across the volcanic belts of Java, North Sumatra, and North Sulawesi — regions that collectively hold over 40% of Indonesia's identified geothermal potential.
