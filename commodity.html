What We Trade

Commodity Products

Agricultural products, forest resources, and industrial minerals sourced responsibly and traded efficiently across domestic and export markets.

Palm Oil

Indonesia is the world's largest producer of palm oil. JPI sources Crude Palm Oil (CPO) and refined palm products from certified plantations in Sumatra and Kalimantan, supplying to food, oleochemical, and biofuel industries.

  • Crude Palm Oil (CPO) & Palm Kernel Oil (PKO)
  • RSPO-certified sustainable sourcing options
  • Refined, Bleached & Deodorized (RBD) palm oil
  • Sources: North Sumatra, Riau, West Kalimantan
  • Export-grade documentation & phytosanitary certs

Natural Rubber

Indonesia ranks among the world's top natural rubber producers. JPI trades high-grade rubber from South Sumatra, Jambi, and Kalimantan, supplying tire manufacturers, industrial rubber processors, and latex product companies.

  • Technically Specified Rubber (TSR / SIR grades)
  • Standard Indonesian Rubber (SIR 3L, SIR 10, SIR 20)
  • Concentrated latex & crepe rubber
  • Sources: South Sumatra, Jambi, West Kalimantan
  • Quality certified by SNI standards

Timber & Wood Products

JPI trades legally verified timber and processed wood products from sustainable forestry operations in Indonesia, serving construction, furniture, and pulp & paper industries domestically and internationally.

  • Sawn timber (meranti, merbau, teak)
  • Plywood & engineered wood panels
  • Wood pellets for biomass energy
  • SVLK (Indonesia TLAS) certified supply chains
  • FSC-certified products available on request

Minerals: Nickel, Bauxite & Tin

Indonesia holds world-class reserves of nickel, bauxite, and tin. JPI facilitates the trading of these critical minerals — vital for EV batteries, aluminum production, and electronics — from Indonesian mining sites to domestic smelters and international buyers.

  • Nickel ore (limonite & saprolite) from Sulawesi & Maluku
  • Nickel pig iron (NPI) & ferronickel trading
  • Bauxite ore from West Kalimantan
  • Refined tin (99.9% Sn) from Bangka Belitung
  • ESDM-IUP licensed supply chains
How We Work

Our Trading Process

A transparent, efficient, and fully documented trading process from initial inquiry to final delivery.

01

Inquiry & Needs Assessment

Client submits requirements — commodity type, grade, quantity, delivery terms, and destination. Our trading team conducts an initial feasibility review.

02

Sourcing & Quotation

JPI matches requirements against our verified supplier network. A formal quotation including pricing, specifications, and delivery timeline is issued within 2–3 business days.

03

Contracting & Compliance

Sales contracts are executed with full legal and regulatory documentation. JPI handles all permitting, export licensing, and compliance requirements on behalf of both parties.

04

Logistics & Delivery

Coordinating with certified logistics partners, JPI manages loading, shipping, and final delivery — with real-time updates and full documentation including COA, B/L, and phytosanitary certificates.
